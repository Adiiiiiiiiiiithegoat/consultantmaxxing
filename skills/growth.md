# Playbook: growth (grow revenue, users, share or a target metric)

## Clarify first
- The metric and target (revenue, DAU, exports, rank) and the time frame.
- Organic only, or inorganic options too? Any constraints (no price change, capex)?
- Current position: segments, geographies, channels, share vs leaders.
- Is it really growth, or a stagnation problem in disguise? (Then use profitability.)

## Structure
Decompose the metric into drivers first, e.g. revenue = customers x revenue per customer
x mix; exports = countries x volume per country; users = acquisition x activation x
retention. Then generate levers per driver:
- Existing products in existing markets (penetration, share, frequency, pricing).
- New markets/segments/geographies for existing products.
- New products/services for existing customers (adjacent, holistic offerings).
- Diversification / inorganic moves, only if organic levers can't reach the target.
Rank levers by impact and feasibility and size the top ones.
- **When it doesn't fit:** if growth stalled, find the bottleneck first (funnel or
  customer-journey analysis) before generating new levers.

## Common patterns (frequency in 6 training cases)
1. A funnel or customer-journey bottleneck: awareness, engagement, payment options,
   distributor capability (3/6).
2. Decompose the target metric and pull the biggest driver (3/6).
3. Shift the mix toward a higher-margin or under-served segment, sized against the target.

## Analyses
Driver tree with current vs required values, `top_down` sizing of new segments,
`scenario_table` across lever combinations, gap-to-target bridge.

## Traps
- Brainstorming levers before decomposing the metric.
- Listing ideas without risks, right-to-win or sizing.
- Not checking whether the target is even reachable with the market size.

## Definition of done
A prioritised set of levers that closes the gap to the target, each sized, with risks,
right to win and a sequence (quick wins vs longer bets).
