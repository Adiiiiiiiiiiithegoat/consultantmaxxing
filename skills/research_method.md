# Skill: research method

- **Research serves the tree.** Every search answers a named branch or hypothesis; stop a
  line of research once its question is answered. Unstructured research is a bottomless
  pit that eats the time needed for synthesis.
- **Source ranking:** filings, regulators, government statistics > established research
  and business press > trade press > aggregators > blogs and forums. Prefer the primary
  source's short results announcement over a full annual report; use the press to find it.
- **Every fact needs:** a source URL, a verbatim quote containing the figure, the entity,
  the metric, the period and the unit. No quote, no fact.
- **Key numbers need two independent sources** (different domains) agreeing within
  tolerance. When sources disagree, keep the range and say so; don't silently pick one.
- **Periods and units are where errors hide:** fiscal vs calendar years, quarterly vs
  annual, standalone vs consolidated, crore/lakh/million/billion, currency. Check the
  table header for the column year.
- **Market data:** published forecasts often give only a target-year value and a growth
  rate; compute the current value as PV = FV / (1 + CAGR)^n, in the sandbox. Sanity-check
  a CAGR against the size and growth of the adjacent traditional category.
- **Estimate only as a last resort**, transparently: factors, formula, rationale for each
  assumption, all recorded in the assumptions register and labelled as estimates.
- **Respect sources:** obey robots.txt, be polite, don't evade bot protections; a blocked
  source is unavailable, not a challenge.
