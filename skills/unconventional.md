# Playbook: unconventional (operations, public sector, puzzles, strategy questions)

## Clarify first
Ask many questions: these problems are defined by their specifics. What does success look
like (a metric and a target)? What is the process, who are the stakeholders, what are the
constraints, short term vs long term?

## Structure
No standard framework fits; build one from the problem's own mechanics:
- Process or journey problems: map the end-to-end process step by step (each actor, each
  handoff, each wait) and find where time, cost or failure accumulates.
- Decisions under uncertainty: list the options, their payoffs and probabilities, and
  compare expected values plus the downside.
- Optimisation puzzles: write the constraint equations and solve for the binding one.
- Stakeholder or strategy questions: list stakeholders and their interests, then design
  options that satisfy the critical ones.
Stay MECE at each level and confirm the structure with the client before going deep.

## Common patterns (frequency in 6 training cases)
1. A process/journey map exposes the bottleneck: turnaround steps, digital access,
   contractual commitments with a platform partner (3/6).
2. Expected-value comparison of options under uncertainty (1/6).
3. A constraint-satisfaction puzzle with an exact answer (1/6).
4. Even spread of the problem across the value chain, requiring a holistic,
   stakeholder-driven strategy (1/6).

## Analyses
Process timing tables, `scenario_table` for expected values, small optimisation scripts
in the sandbox, stakeholder-interest matrices.

## Traps
- Forcing a standard profitability/entry framework onto it.
- Not stepping into the user's shoes: real-world operational details are often the key.
- Missing contractual or partner-side causes (look outside the client too).

## Definition of done
The mechanism or best option is identified with the numbers that prove it, plus
short-term and long-term actions and how success will be measured.
