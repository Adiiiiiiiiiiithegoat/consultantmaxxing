# Playbook: M&A / private equity (should we buy, invest in, or merge with X)

Only one training case of this type exists, so this playbook leans on general practice.

## Clarify first
- The investor's objective and thesis (growth, synergies, capability, return target and
  holding period), and constraints (budget, control, timeline).
- Target: business, market position, financials; is the question the industry or the
  specific company?

## Structure
1. Market / industry attractiveness: size, growth, profitability, competition, entry
   barriers, regulation, supply-chain constraints, technology risk.
2. Target quality: position, differentiation, management, unit economics, financial
   health.
3. Synergies (revenue and cost) and integration feasibility (strategic only).
4. Valuation and returns: price vs intrinsic value, IRR/multiple over the holding period.
5. Risks, deal structure, exit options.

## Common patterns
- Attractiveness can be decided by a supply constraint (raw materials, enabling
  infrastructure) rather than demand (the one training case).
- Synergies are usually overestimated; haircut them and phase them in.

## Analyses
`npv`, `irr`, `payback_period`, `sensitivity` on growth, margin and exit multiple;
synergy build-up; market sizing for the target's segment.

## Traps
- Recommending a deal without a price or return logic.
- Treating industry attractiveness as target attractiveness.
- Ignoring integration cost and timing.

## Definition of done
Invest / don't invest (and at what price or structure), the return case with the key
sensitivities, top risks and conditions to proceed.
